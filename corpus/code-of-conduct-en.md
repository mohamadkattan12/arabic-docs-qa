# Code of Conduct - Mithal Co. (fictional document for demo purposes)

## Gifts

Employees may accept gifts from clients or suppliers worth up to **SAR 300**. Any gift above that must
be declared to Compliance within 5 working days and may need to be returned. Cash gifts are never allowed.

## Conflicts of interest

Employees must declare any outside job, board seat, or investment above 5% in a supplier or competitor.
The declaration form is renewed every January.

## Speaking up

Concerns can be raised with a manager, HR, or the anonymous ethics line at ethics@mithal.example.
Retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith is a disciplinary offense.

## Social media

Employees must not share confidential information or speak on behalf of the company on social media
without approval from the Communications team.

## Disciplinary steps

Violations follow four steps: verbal warning, written warning, final written warning, termination.
Serious violations (fraud, harassment, data theft) can lead directly to termination.
