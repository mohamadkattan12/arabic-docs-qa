# Remote Work Policy - Mithal Co. (fictional document for demo purposes)

## Eligibility

Employees who have completed their 90-day probation may work remotely. Roles that require
on-site equipment (lab, reception, facilities) are not eligible.

## Allowance

Eligible employees may work remotely up to **two (2) days per week**. Tuesday is a mandatory
office day for all teams. Fully remote arrangements need written approval from the department director
and are reviewed every six months.

## Working from abroad

Working from outside Saudi Arabia is allowed for a maximum of **20 working days per calendar year**,
and requires approval from HR at least 10 working days in advance, for tax and data-protection reasons.

## Equipment and costs

The company provides a laptop and one external monitor. A one-time home-office allowance of
**SAR 1,200** can be claimed within the first 3 months of starting remote work.

## Availability

Remote employees must be reachable on the company chat during core hours (10:00 to 15:00) and must
keep their camera on for team meetings. Client data may only be accessed over the company VPN.
