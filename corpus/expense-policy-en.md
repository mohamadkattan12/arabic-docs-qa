# Expense Policy - Mithal Co. (fictional document for demo purposes)

## Submitting claims

Expense claims must be submitted in the finance system within **45 days** of the expense date, with an
itemized receipt for every item above SAR 50. Claims older than 45 days are rejected.

## Approval limits

- Up to SAR 2,000: approved by the direct manager.
- SAR 2,001 to SAR 10,000: also needs the department director.
- Above SAR 10,000: also needs the CFO.

## Meals

Business meals with clients are reimbursed up to **SAR 250 per person**. Team meals need prior approval
and are limited to one per quarter. Alcohol is never reimbursed.

## Reimbursement time

Approved claims are paid with the next payroll if approved before the 20th of the month; otherwise with
the following payroll.

## Corporate card

Employees who travel more than 4 times a year can request a corporate card. Personal use of the
corporate card is not allowed; personal charges must be repaid within 7 days.
