# Business Travel Policy - Mithal Co. (fictional document for demo purposes)

## Booking

All flights and hotels are booked through the company travel desk at least **10 days** before departure.
Bookings made directly by employees are only reimbursed with written approval from the travel desk.

## Flight class

Economy class for flights under **6 hours**. Business class is allowed for flights of 6 hours or more,
and for the CEO and directors on any flight.

## Hotels

Hotel limit: **SAR 900 per night** in Riyadh, Jeddah and Dammam, and **SAR 1,400 per night** abroad.

## Daily allowance (per diem)

- Inside Saudi Arabia: SAR 350 per day.
- Abroad: SAR 600 per day.
The per diem covers meals and local transport; receipts are not required for it.

## Travel insurance

Travel insurance is provided automatically for every trip booked through the travel desk, and covers
medical emergencies up to SAR 500,000.
